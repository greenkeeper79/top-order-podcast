The team

A fearsome Top Order with precisely zero first-class runs between us.

B

Binksy

Co-host

Hailing from Birmingham, England and now back living in England after years in Auckland, I combined limited cricketing talent with a lot of luck to play with and against some greats of the game. I spent over 10 years in Birmingham Premier League cricket, rubbing shoulders with the likes of Wasim Akram, Andre Russell, Kevin Pietersen and Ian Bell.

A season of Tauranga Prem cricket in 2002/3 is what first took me to New Zealand, and I still have a soft spot for the place. I’m now back in England with my amazing wife and two boys, who want to play Test cricket against each other – the eldest claims to be English, the other Kiwi.

B

Baldy

Co-host and resident statistician

Hailing from North Queensland, Australia and known for giving my legbreak an almighty rip, I’m the Australian you can hear every week. Despite the occasional dig at the Aussie team (mostly by me), I’m still loving the show.

I bring the numbers when there hasn’t been enough talk of averages and strike rates, and my legacy to cricket will (with any luck) be the stats and analysis in our Hall of Fame countdowns.

L

Lippy

Co-host

Born in Norfolk, England but raised in New Zealand, I’m probably the most one-eyed cricket fan of the group. You’ll hear me praising the Black Caps, working Riccarton Cricket Club into the conversation, or recalling the time an off-spinner spun one through the gate.

It’s been a while since I stepped on a cricket field in anger, but I played with and against many international cricketers in my club days and loved testing myself against the best.

R

Raj

Co-host and producer

Born and raised on the mean streets of Auckland’s North Shore, I push the buttons and try to get away with a dad joke or two. I loved playing when I was younger and now love watching, and you’ll often hear me on the administrative side of the game.

“I am jealous of my parents. I will never have a kid as cool as theirs.” – Chris Gayle, the Universe Boss

J

Dr Jamie Barker

Sport psychology, Loughborough University

Jamie is a Reader in Applied Sport and Performance Psychology at Loughborough University and a Chartered Sport and Exercise Psychologist, with more than 20 years working with professional cricket, football and elite performers. He’s co-author of The Psychology of Cricket.

He played British Universities cricket, so he knows what it’s like out in the middle, and he’s a mad Manchester United fan, so he knows plenty about pressure and heartbreak too.

On the show Jamie brings the mindset behind the results: pressure, confidence and why teams turn around so quickly. He also provides unofficial therapy to the rest of us, free of charge and sorely needed.
